Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 2025

DocPolish Utility ("the Add-on") processes document data strictly inside your active Google account session. We do not store, log, or transmit any user data, document contents, or personal information to any external server.

Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance:
DocPolish Utility's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data Scopes Requested:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/documents.currentonly — Allows the add-on to view and format only the document where you explicitly run the tool.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/script.locale — Used to format dates and numbers according to your preferred language and locale.

User Guide & Support

  1. Open any Google Doc and launch DocPolish Utility from the right-hand companion sidebar.
  2. Readability Stats: Click "Refresh Doc Stats" to calculate words, characters, and reading/speaking times.
  3. Whitespace Cleaner: Click "Clean Extra Spaces" to eliminate double spaces and empty lines.
  4. Smart Case Converter: Highlight text in your document and choose UPPERCASE, lowercase, or Title Case.
  5. Meeting Template: Click "Insert Meeting Header" to paste a structured agenda block at your cursor.
